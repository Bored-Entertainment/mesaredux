DMCA POLICY
Last Updated: [11/17/2025]

Bored Entertainment respects the intellectual property rights of game creators and
content owners.
We host all of our games on our servers.

1. Reporting Copyright Infringement

If you believe your copyrighted content appears on our site without permission,
please send a removal request through our Contact page including:

    Your full name
    Contact information
    A link to the copyrighted work
    A link to the game or page where the content appears on our site
    A statement that you are the copyright owner or are authorized to act on their behalf

We will respond within 1 business week.

2. Good Faith Removal

If the claim is valid, we will remove or disable access to the content promptly.

3. Repeat Violations

We reserve the right to remove content or block access for repeated copyright complaints.

4. Counter-Notice

If you believe your content was removed in error, you may submit a counter-notice with:

    Your name
    Contact info
    The removed link
    A statement explaining why removal was incorrect

We will review the claim and respond in a timely manner.

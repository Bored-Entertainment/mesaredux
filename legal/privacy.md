We have compiled this Privacy Policy to inform you how your personal information is collected, used, and protected when you interact with **Bored Entertainment** on our website [MESλREDUX](https://mesaredux.com). Personally identifiable information (PII) is information that can be used to identify, contact, or locate an individual. Please read carefully to understand our practices regarding your personal information.  

### Information We Collect

We may collect your name, email address, phone number, mailing address, or other relevant information when you submit forms, respond to surveys, or interact with the website in other ways.  

This information may be used for purposes including:  

- Personalizing your experience on our website  
- Improving our website and services  
- Responding to customer service requests  
- Processing transactions efficiently  
- Sending updates about services, improvements, and new products  


### Protection of Your Information

We implement regular scans to detect security vulnerabilities and maintain a secure network. Sensitive information, such as credit card data, is encrypted using Secure Socket Layer (SSL) technology. Access to personal data is limited to authorized personnel only.  


### Cookies and Tracking

For detailed information about how we use cookies, please see our [Cookies Page]({{ '/legal/cookies' | relative_url }}). In summary:  

- We primarily use **Google Analytics** to gather aggregated website usage data.  
- We also use **local storage** for site features such as persistent messages or preferences.  
- You can manage or disable cookies through your browser, but some features of our site may not function properly.  
- **Bored Entertainment is not responsible for tracking performed by third-party games or content hosted on our site**, which may collect information in ways we cannot control or detect.  


### Third-Party Information

We do not sell or transfer your personal information to third parties. If disclosure is necessary by law or to protect rights, we will do so responsibly.  

Third-party products or games hosted on our site may have their own privacy practices. **Bored Entertainment is not liable for these practices.**  


### California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA)

In compliance with CalOPPA, we provide the following:  

- Users can visit our site anonymously.  
- A link to this Privacy Policy is included on our home page and significant entry pages.  
- Users will be notified of any Privacy Policy changes via email.
